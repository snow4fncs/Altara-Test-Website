Class

Which class

Adventure machines are tall, heavy and comfortable, and are the only class permitted on unsealed roads. Naked machines are the lightest and quickest to turn, with no luggage beyond a tank bag. Touring means two people and 600 km days. Sport means a committed riding position and a morning on one good road. Classic means low seats, easy engines and the coast at 80 km/h.

If you have not ridden for a few years, start with the Bonneville T120 or the R 1250 GS.
