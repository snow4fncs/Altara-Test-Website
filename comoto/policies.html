Policies, in plain English

These are the terms you agree to when you hold dates. They are written to be read, not skimmed past. The hire agreement you sign at pickup says the same things in the same words.

Who can hire

Your licence is checked against the physical card at pickup, and against the RMS or equivalent register where we are able to. A licence that is suspended, expired or does not match the rider is a cancelled booking with no refund. We would rather say that here than at the counter.

Insurance and excess

The policy covers damage to the machine, damage to third party property and third party injury while the machine is ridden lawfully by the named rider within the terms below. If the machine is damaged

Tell us at return, or by phone straight away if it affects safety. We photograph the damage with you, against the condition photographs taken at pickup. We obtain a repair quote from the servicing garage, show it to you, and charge the lesser of the quote and your excess to the card on file. If the repair takes the machine out of the fleet for more than three days we may charge loss of use at 50% of the day rate for each further day, capped at ten days. You receive the invoice for the repair.

Existing damage is photographed and published on every machine's page with a date. You are never charged for something that was already there.

If you cancel

Refunds go to the original card within five business days. Dates can be moved once at no cost if you tell us more than 48 hours before pickup and the new dates are free. If we cancel, for a fault with the machine or a road closure that makes the hire impossible, you receive a full refund or the closest equivalent machine at the same rate, your choice.

Privacy

We collect your name, contact details, licence details and card details because we cannot hire you a motorcycle without them. Licence images are stored encrypted, checked by a person at pickup, and deleted 30 days after your return. Card details are held by and never touch our systems. We do not sell, share or rent your details to anyone. We email you about your booking and, if you tick the box, about the fleet twice a year at most. You can ask for everything we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, by emailing garage@comotoclub.com.au and we will do it within ten business days.

On the road

Ride to the law. Fines and camera infringements are transferred to the rider by statutory declaration, plus a $50 administration fee per notice. The machines are tracked for recovery in the event of theft and the location data is not looked at for any other reason. If you are stopped by police, the rego papers and our phone number are in the tank bag.
