How it works

Included days are any machine, any day of the week, and they roll for the full membership year. Days beyond your allowance are charged at the day rate less your tier's discount. The booking window is how far ahead you can hold dates. Day hire is 30 days. Guest days let a licensed friend ride a second machine on your membership, under the same eligibility rules and with their own licence checked at pickup. Bond figures are the authorisation held on your card for each hire. Excess is the same at every tier, and excess reduction is priced the same at every tier.

Membership is paid annually and is not refundable after the first hire. It can be paused once for up to three months for injury or travel. Ask.
