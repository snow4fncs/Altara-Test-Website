Before you go

Check Live Traffic NSW the morning you leave. Mountain roads close for repairs and after weather with little warning. Wildlife is at its worst in the hour after dawn and the hour before dusk, which is when the light is best and when most of our claims happen. Difficulty describes the road, not the rider. Choose the machine that suits the road.
