About Comoto Club

Comoto Club is for riders with a full licence and a free weekend who want to ride a machine they would not otherwise own, on a road they have not ridden.

Why it exists

Most riders who can afford a good machine cannot justify owning one they use twelve times a year, and most hire fleets are old and tired. So this fleet is small, under two years old, and replaced before it is three. Every machine is serviced by the same mechanic, and the service record is published on its page.

The people

Names and photographs are added when confirmed. We do not publish stock portraits.

The premises

Machines are stored inside, under cover, on tenders. Nothing lives outdoors. Pickup and return happen under cover, in light good enough to photograph the condition of the machine properly, together. Where that is, and the photographs of it, are added once the premises are confirmed.
