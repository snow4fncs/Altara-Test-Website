How booking works

Nothing is charged until you hold. Eligibility is checked at step two, not at the counter.

  1. Hold your dates

    Pick a machine and dates. Held dates are yours for 24 hours while you finish the rest.

  2. Send your licence

    Front and back, uploaded once. A person checks it against the card at pickup, and the file is deleted 30 days after you return.

  3. Choose your cover

    Standard excess or reduced. Pillion, comms, delivery. The total updates as you go, GST included, no line hidden.

  4. Collect and ride

    The bike is fuelled, inspected against a 20 item checklist and waiting. Sign, photograph the odometer together, ride.

Maintenance

One mechanic looks after all nine machines. Nothing is sent out to a dealer.

Every machine is serviced at 5,000 km or six months, whichever arrives first, by , who also signs the pre-hire checklist before a key leaves the building. Tyres come off at 3 mm of centre tread, not at the legal 1.5 mm, because a hire bike gets ridden in weather the owner would have stayed home for. Brake pads are measured with a gauge at every return and the depth is written into the logbook you can read on each machine's page. Chains are cleaned and adjusted after every hire, not every service. Oil is changed on the interval regardless of how clean it looks.

The pre-hire inspection is the same 20 checks every time, signed and photographed. It takes about 40 minutes. You are welcome to watch.
