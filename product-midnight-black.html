Before you ask

Straight answers.

Does it fit my car?

The cover is engineered for a universal fit across the vast majority of standard passenger seats — 145cm × 70cm, with MagFit tension adjusting to seat width and depth. One size fits most, from hatchbacks to utes.

Will it interfere with my seat airbag?

It is designed to be compatible with most side-impact seat airbags. If your seats have integrated side airbags, check your owner’s manual before fitting any aftermarket cover.

How does it stay on the seat?

MagFit magnetic snap-on clips secure the cover behind the seat back — no tools, no straps, no modification. The magnets are tuned to hold through daily driving, hard braking included, and the anti-slip rubber base keeps the seat panel planted.

How long does fitting take?

Under 30 seconds per seat. Drop it over the headrest, smooth the base outward from the centre, clip the MagFit magnets behind the seat back. Fully reversible — removal leaves no marks or residue.

Can I machine wash it?

Yes — cold, gentle cycle. Remove the clips first, hang dry. Wash every 4–8 weeks in normal use, or more often after the gym or beach.

Does it work with heated seats?

Yes. The microfibre is thin enough to let heat through with only a slight reduction in efficiency. Not recommended over integrated massage seats.
