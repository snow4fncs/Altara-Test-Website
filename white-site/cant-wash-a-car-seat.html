You can’t wash
a car seat.

Seven things that end up on it anyway, why a towel does not fix it, and the $89 cover that comes off and goes in the machine.

Written by the Altara team · 2 minute read · Australian owned, ships from Sydney

What ends up on the seat

  1. Sweat, after the gym or the surf

    Sweat is salt, oil and bacteria. It soaks into fabric, sits under leather stitching, and the smell comes back every warm day. A detailer charges around $55 a seat to lift it, and it comes back the next session.

  2. Sand

    It gets into the seams and the seat rails, and it scratches leather every time you shift. You can vacuum the seat for ten minutes and still feel it on the back of your legs.

  3. Dog hair, and wet dog

    Hair weaves into fabric and the vacuum does not get it. Wet dog goes straight through a towel and into the foam, which is where the smell lives.

  4. Kids

    Juice, biscuits, the yoghurt pouch, the toilet-training accident. Most of it lands on the seat, not the mat.

  5. Coffee

    The one that goes over at the roundabout. Milk in fabric is a smell you cannot get out.

  6. Work gear

    Hi-vis dye, dust, grease off your hands. Tradies wear through a driver’s seat years before the rest of the car.

  7. Sunscreen and fake tan

    Both stain leather, and both show on light interiors within a week.

Why a towel does not work

Everyone tries the beach towel first. It slides off by the second roundabout, it soaks straight through, and it looks like a towel on a seat. The most common complaint on every seat-cover review site in Australia is not the dirt. It is covers and towels that slide, bunch and need re-tucking every time you get in.

Why fitted covers do not either

Custom canvas and neoprene sets run $200 to $330, take the better part of an hour to fit, and then they are on for good. They protect the seat by hiding it, and they cannot go in the wash.

What we built instead

A seat cover that goes in the washing machine.

  • Microfibre towelling on top, waterproof backing underneath. Nothing reaches the seat.
  • Magnets sewn into the strap snap behind the seat back, and a grip base holds it under you. No straps, no hooks, no sliding off.
  • On in 30 seconds. Off in ten. Cold wash, hang dry, back on.
  • Two front covers, $89, delivered free with Australia Post. Four covers for the full car, $159.
  • 60-day fit guarantee. Wrong fit, full refund.
