Altara poly bag — print specification

15 October 2026 · Altara Design, Sydney · hello@altaradesign.com · +61 420 235 400

Bag

Clear frosted zip-lock poly bag, the same bag currently used for the seat covers, approximately 270 mm wide × 350 mm tall. If your bag size differs, scale the artwork to keep the logo at 96 mm wide and the warning text at 10 pt. Nothing prints on the zip strip.

Print

ColourOne colour only: opaque white. No other colours.
FrontAltara script logo, 96 mm wide, centred left to right, top of logo 118 mm from the top edge of the bag. Nothing else on the front.
BackWarning block, 120 mm wide, centred left to right, 22 mm from the bottom edge. Text as supplied, 10 pt, do not shrink. Small line "ALTARA · SYDNEY · ALTARADESIGN.COM" beneath it.
Filesbag-artwork-front-back.pdf (1:1, two pages, vector, black background shows position only and is NOT printed). altara-script-white.svg (logo as vector).
MethodFlexographic or screen print, whichever you use for one-colour bag printing. Please send a photo of one printed bag before the full run.

Why the warning

Required by Amazon for any poly bag with an opening over 12.7 cm. The wording is Amazon's accepted text and must not be altered.
