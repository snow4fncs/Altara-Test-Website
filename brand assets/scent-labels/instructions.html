3. Printing

  1. Print each PDF at 100% scale. Every file already includes 1.5 mm bleed on all sides. Cut to the trim sizes above (bottle 22 × 22 mm, box 52 × 68 mm).
  2. Flat colour only. No metallic, no foil, no texture. Print the coloured square and the coloured scent name in CMYK exactly as in the file.
  3. Material: black vinyl sticker, matte finish, with a matte clear laminate on top. No gloss.
  4. Do not add anything: no border, no barcode, no logo, no text.
  5. Colour accuracy is the most important thing. The dark red (Leather) and the green (Pineapple & Birch) must not look pink or bright green.

4. Before the full run

Print ONE bottle sticker and ONE box sticker of the Leather design, apply them to a bottle and a box, and send a photo. We will confirm within 24 hours, then please run all 200.
